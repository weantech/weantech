01

Behavioral Security
and Insider Threat

The human element is the hardest problem.

Most organizations have reasonable perimeter controls. Few have a credible answer for the threat that already has access. WeanTech designs behavioral detection architectures that surface psychological escalation, drift, and intent before a technical event occurs.

Delivered under the Opaque Clarity Security Framework, this work draws directly from active research in federated neural architectures and behavioral indicator modeling.

What This Looks Like in Practice

  • Behavioral indicator frameworks tailored to your environment
  • Insider threat detection architecture and tooling
  • Threat modeling that accounts for human factors, not just attack vectors
  • Detection logic grounded in General Strain Theory and moral disengagement research

This work is the applied core of the Opaque Clarity Security Framework: defending the asymmetry when the threat is already inside the perimeter.

What This Looks Like in Practice

  • Hardening baselines grounded in operational reality, not compliance checklists
