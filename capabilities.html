WeanTech LLC · Field Work

Independent evaluation. Practical resilience.

Field work funds and informs the research program. WeanTech does not sell generic AI implementation. We help organizations evaluate what is being proposed, understand what fails under disruption, and design field studies that produce usable evidence.

01

AI & Operational
Assurance Review

Independent scrutiny before—or after—an AI decision.

An AI proposal should be judged by the workflow, data, security, governance, failure modes, and people who will rely on it—not by a sales demonstration. WeanTech provides an independent technical assessment before a major commitment or when a deployed system is not delivering what was promised.

Typical Output

  • Problem-fit, data, security, and vendor-dependency review
  • Human-control, auditability, and failure-mode assessment
  • Plain-language risk and remediation priorities
  • No vendor commissions and no default implementation agenda
02

Resilience Readiness
Assessment

Understand what remains when dependencies fail.

Organizations often discover their real dependencies during an outage or incident. This engagement maps the relationship between cloud services, identity, networks, vendors, physical operations, and decision workflows—then identifies practical degraded-mode options.

The aim is not a generic compliance checklist. It is a clear picture of the operational consequences of failure and a prioritized plan for retaining useful capability.

Typical Output

  • Dependency and failure-cascade map
  • Cyber-physical risk and continuity observations
  • Degraded-mode workflow priorities
  • Evidence-backed next-step recommendations

The work is guided by our research principles: degraded-mode first, security by design, human accountability, and evidence over hype.

03

Field Exercise &
Evaluation Study

Test the operating concept before trusting it.

WeanTech designs and documents scenario-based exercises for emerging communications, continuity, and decision-support capabilities. The objective is to learn what works, what fails, and what conditions must be true—not to stage a demonstration that guarantees a favorable result.

Typical Output

  • Scenario design, safety boundaries, and measurable test objectives
  • Prototype or operating-concept observation
  • After-action report with limitations and findings
  • A responsible basis for the next research or deployment decision
Research-Led Model

Services are a means, not the identity.

Each field engagement must either strengthen operational resilience for the client or contribute a legitimate lesson to the research program—ideally both. WeanTech does not use field work to funnel organizations toward a predetermined AI tool or platform.
