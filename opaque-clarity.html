Our operating principles.

WeanTech develops capabilities for conditions where time, communications, and certainty are limited. That work demands a higher standard than a polished demonstration. These principles guide the research, prototype, and field-evaluation process.

Degraded-mode first. Core capability should remain useful without Internet, cloud access, or a healthy primary network.

Secure by design. Identity, authorization, encryption, and auditability belong in the system from the beginning.

Human accountable. AI may assist a bounded task; people retain authority, can override it, and can understand the basis for material recommendations.

Evidence over hype. We state the operating conditions, test the limits, publish what happened, and distinguish a prototype from a deployable system.

Interoperable where possible. Systems should avoid unnecessary dependence on a single vendor, platform, or opaque data model.
