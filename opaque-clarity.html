Opacity for adversaries. Clarity for defenders. By design.

Asymmetric visibility.

Opaque Clarity is a security design philosophy built on the principle of asymmetric visibility. Optimization for defenders who see everything, and adversaries who see nothing useful.

The insider threat is not a network problem with a human component. It is a human problem with a network component.
