The Problem

Behavior over bytes.

Security architecture assumes the threat is external. When it isn't, most systems have no answer. WeanTech builds for the harder case, where the adversary already has access, where drift precedes breach, and where psychology matters as much as protocol.

The behavioral dimension of security, understanding the why behind technical failures and not just the mechanics, is where the real gaps are. And where our research is pointed.

The hardest case for any security framework is when the threat originates inside the perimeter. Behavioral security is how that case gets answered.
