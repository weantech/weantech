The Research Question

What must still work when ordinary infrastructure cannot be assumed?

Disasters, outages, cyber incidents, and vendor failures expose the dependencies hidden in everyday operations. WeanTech researches practical systems that preserve communication, situational awareness, and sound human judgment when those dependencies break.

The work begins at the physical-cyber edge: where networks, security, people, and real-world consequences meet. AI may assist a clearly bounded task, but it is never the mission and never the sole path to a working system.

Responsible technology should be rapidly deployable, secure by design, interoperable where possible, and useful under stress.

Research Portfolio

One active program. One emerging line of inquiry.

WeanTech is beginning with practical, testable problems rather than a catalog of speculative products. Project MESH is the active research demonstrator; adjacent themes define where the work can lead, and a new line of inquiry is emerging around autonomous systems operating under adversarial conditions.

Active Prototype

Project MESH

Can a small team establish secure local communications in 15 minutes or less without cellular, Internet, or cloud dependencies?

Research Theme

Resilient Operations

How can organizations retain useful operational capability during the loss of cloud, identity, connectivity, or a critical vendor?

Research Theme

Human-Controlled Decision Support

How can technology help operators make decisions under uncertainty while keeping authority, accountability, and override with people?

Emerging Research

Adversarial Autonomous Systems

How should autonomous agents be evaluated when environments are dynamic, partially observable, and actively adapt to the agent’s behavior?
